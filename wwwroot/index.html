PIMS User Manual

Operating Modes

The system classifies biological events into four distinct operational modes to maintain accurate statistical tracking:

  • Maintenance: Standard self-regulated cycle clearing. Used to establish baseline recovery metrics and enforce system turnover.
  • Playtime: Recreational relational events without reproductive intent. Tracked for system strain analysis but excluded from strict yield optimization.
  • Baby-Making: Active reproductive attempts. The system auto-estimates volume based on historical recovery gap and saturation metrics.
  • Clinical-Lab: Ground-truth baseline data. Used to record lab analytics (Concentration, Motility) to verify system integrity and clear Thermal Shadows.

    • Thermal Shadows

    Spermatogenesis operates on a 74-day biological cycle. Severe thermal exposure (fevers, hot tubs, saunas) critically degrades system integrity during this window.

  • Logging a Heat Event (Level 2 or 3) instantly triggers a Thermal Shadow warning that persists for exactly 74 days.
  • Clinical Baseline testing is highly discouraged during a shadow, as the data will be artificially depressed and inaccurate.
  • A shadow can be overridden early if a Clinical Baseline test is logged with passing metrics (≥15 M/mL Conc and ≥40% Motility) after the heat event occurred.

    • Biological Saturation Index

    Supplements like Zinc, Maca, and Vitamins D/C do not provide instantaneous benefits. The system tracks Saturation over rolling time windows.

  • A supplement is considered "Saturated" if it was logged during at least 50% of the events within its specific biological buildup window (e.g., 21 days for Zinc, 30 days for D3).
  • The Analyze Supps tool in Settings calculates the exact yield and recovery time differences between Saturated and Un-Saturated system states.

    • Stealth Mode (Panic Key)

    To instantly obfuscate all sensitive biological data, charts, and table logs from screen-lookers:

  • Press Escape to blur the UI. Press it again to clear.
